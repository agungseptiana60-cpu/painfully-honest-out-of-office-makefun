# The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office

One brutally honest but still polite out-of-office auto-reply, for the make.fun bounty
[The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-the-painfully-honest-out-of-office-63e7ca0b).

> I'm out of the office and, honestly, relieved — your email joins a queue I'll face with fresh dread when I'm back, and I'll answer it properly then; thank you for waiting.

`main.ts` writes that single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. The honesty is the
relief at being away and the dread at the queue waiting back; the politeness is
the promise to answer properly and the thanks.
